Acme Team Handbook (fictional company, for evaluation only)
Every employee at Acme receives 24 days of paid leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 10 days in advance through the HR portal.
Probation for new employees lasts 3 months.

Remote Work and Equipment
Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week after probation ends.
Acme provides one laptop and a monitor budget of 300 US dollars for home office setup.
The official work chat tool at Acme is called TeamChat, and official email uses the acme.example domain.
